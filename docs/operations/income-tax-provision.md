# Current income-tax provision

`/app/tax/provision` provides a provisional, single-jurisdiction current-tax estimate. It does not calculate deferred tax, allocate income across jurisdictions, file a T2/GST/HST return, plan instalments or initiate payments. There are no seeded legal rates, province defaults or assumed CCPC/small-business eligibility.

Configure the exact entity, ledger, fiscal year and start/end dates. Record corporation status, income category, eligibility evidence, enacted rule versions with authoritative HTTPS references and effective dates, ascending bands ending in an unlimited band, explicit loss/credit amounts (including zero), account mappings and completeness evidence. Reviewed annual rates are weighted by days across the fiscal tax year. Proposed or unknown legislation and gaps/overlaps block readiness. Rate bands and business-limit allocation must be reviewed for this entity and income category; this is not an automatic eligibility engine.

Save assumptions creates a new immutable draft revision; Review captures exactly that version. Any change requires another review. `tax.filing.configuration.manage` controls these operations. Prior inputs and sources remain inspectable.

Preview starts with posted functional-currency revenue less expenses, excluding all explicitly identified current-tax expense accounts. It adds separately evidenced permanent/temporary adjustments and the exact fiscal-year lines from selected current asset-tax schedules. Each selected CCA claim must be within that fiscal-year line's reviewed claim. Book depreciation is added back net of posted reversals; the explicit YTD CCA claim is deducted. Superseded schedules/classifications, missing fiscal-year lines and duplicate asset selection block readiness. Review completeness of the selected assets and other adjustments; the model does not infer missing claims. Fiscal coverage gaps and outstanding non-provision journals are blockers. The bounded journal window is explicit and blocks approval above 10,000 journals.

Taxable income is floored at zero after supported losses. Nonrefundable credits are capped at gross current tax. Neither losses nor credits create refundable assets. Prior current-year posted provision expenses, including linked full reversals, reduce the incremental adjustment. Existing tax-expense journals require evidence-backed classification. Earlier-year true-ups and cash settlements remain separate. The liability roll-forward includes opening balance and actual posted movements; unclassified movements remain visible rather than being presumed instalments.

Save calculation freezes source IDs, hashes, rule/assumption versions, dates, mappings, bridge, warnings and liability roll-forward. Identical sources reuse the same calculation. Review and generation require fresh sources and `tax.filings.prepare`; generation additionally requires `ledger.journal.draft`. Zero adjustments record an explicit no-entry decision. Nonzero adjustments create two exact balanced functional-currency lines and remain DRAFT even under AUTO_POST. There is one generation decision per calculation, and one outstanding provision draft per ledger/tax year. Retries return the original event/journal even after posting or subsequent period closure.

Posting is a separate ordinary journal operation, subject to normal approval, permission and period controls. A database guard rejects posting after relevant posted books, assumptions, CCA schedules or source completeness change. Generated lines and accounting inputs cannot be edited. Resolve a stale unposted draft through the existing authorized journal administration workflow, then save/review a fresh calculation. Posted corrections use normal controlled reversal and a new reviewed calculation. No operation silently reopens a period or moves dates.

Web and MCP use the same services. New tools list provisions, preview, configure assumptions, save calculations and review/generate drafts; existing permission keys are reused without grants. Tables have forced tenant RLS, append-only guards, actor/permission checks, scope foreign keys, expected-version checks, idempotency and business audit. Read paths do not create journals or alter accounting.

Validation uses synthetic rates and isolated tenants: zero/loss/positive and day-weighted/banded arithmetic, full PostgreSQL review/generation/posting/reversal/cash/true-up flows, stale-source and closed-period rejection, concurrent revisions, immutable snapshots, denied actors/tenants, and Chromium interactions with production components. An external client and hosted UI must be checked after deployment before ticket closure.
