# Supplier invoice rounding and reviewed zero invoices

Supplier bills accept `lines[].tax.sourceTaxRounding` with the original signed `amount`, `evidenceReference`, review `reason`, and explicit `reviewed: true`. UI users select **Match verified invoice tax rounding** on the affected line. The existing `tax.determinations.override` permission is required for creating, editing, or issuing the bill. A pending review can be saved but cannot be posted.

The source amount may differ from the automated tax by at most one minor unit of the invoice currency per line. Original net amounts, tax jurisdiction/rates, component account mappings, and recovery proportions remain intact. The snapshot records the automated amount, original source tax, exact difference, evidence, reason, and review state. Larger differences require the existing separately reviewed source-tax override; they cannot use this rounding path. For example, CAD 85.00 with 11.05 tax and a -33.87 cancellation with printed -4.41 tax produce net 51.13, tax 6.64, and payable 57.77. Draft evidence checks, posting, journal tax lines, and open items use those same totals.

For a verified purchase invoice whose original total is zero, complete the inbox item with `action.type: "REVIEWED_NO_ACCOUNTING"`, `confirmed: true`, and a treatment of `PREPAID_USAGE_REVIEWED_SEPARATELY`, `OFFSETTING_CREDITS`, or `OTHER_REVIEWED_ZERO`. Keep its original `PURCHASE_INVOICE` document type, date, reference, currency, counterparty, zero total, and source checksum, and provide a review reason of at least eight characters. This creates an evidence asset and an encrypted review record; it creates no bill, journal, open item, settlement, or prepaid amortization. A separate accounting review may still be needed for recognition or amortization.

An optional `relatedEvidence` can identify the exact current supplier purchase (`SOURCE_DOCUMENT`, `sourceDocumentId`, `expectedVersion`, `expectedContentHash`) or a posted journal (`JOURNAL`, `journalId`, `expectedContentHash`) in the same company. This is a supporting reference, so its date and amount may differ. Journal links require ledger-read permission. The original invoice remains distinct from receipt/settlement evidence. The inbox displays its completed no-new-payable review reason.

Completion binds the claim, source checksum, metadata, review, and related version in one immutable completion hash. Exact retries recover filing without repeating accounting; changed arguments conflict. Duplicate source bytes or invoice reference/counterparty/currency are held for review. Nonzero invoices still require the ordinary invoice workflow. Existing immutable source and journal snapshots are not rewritten.

The browser rounding regression is opt-in with `E2E_SOURCE_TAX_REVIEW_ENABLED=true` against an isolated seeded database with an explicitly authorized synthetic tax reviewer. Public demo accountant roles do not receive tax-override privileges as part of this change. Ordinary release acceptance leaves this reviewer-only case skipped.
# Ontario insurance RST source review

For an invoice that separately states Ontario insurance retail sales tax, preserve the premium net and printed RST as separate source facts. Ontario describes an 8% RST on taxable insurance premiums; it also notes that a premium portion subject to HST is exempt from RST. Review the actual invoice and coverage before selecting this treatment ([Ontario insurance RST guidance](https://www.ontario.ca/document/retail-sales-tax/insurance-and-benefits-plans)).

For a verified CAD 100.00 premium plus CAD 8.00 RST, create a supplier-bill draft with one CAD 100.00 line using `ca.on.hst`, `category: EXEMPT`, Ontario destination and `recoverablePercent: "0"`. Set `sourceTaxOverride` to `ratePercent: "8"`, `amount: "8.00"`, `jurisdiction: "CA-ON"`, `componentKey: "ON_INSURANCE_RST"`, an effective date, a reason and the original evidence reference. Leave `reviewedTreatment` absent. The resulting immutable draft records CAD 100.00 net, CAD 8.00 source tax and CAD 108.00 gross, but cannot be issued yet.

Use `finlynq_daily_get_source_tax_review_access` to discover whether the current member has `tax.determinations.override`. An authorized member should read the existing document and source evidence, then call `finlynq_daily_edit_supplier_bill` on the exact current DRAFT version with the same financial facts and `sourceTaxOverride.reviewedTreatment: NONRECOVERABLE`. The reviewed component is capitalized with the line cost; no recoverable GST/HST component is created. Issuing still requires the normal payables and journal posting permissions. A member without review permission can save the pending draft and hand its source number and version to a reviewer. Repeated create/edit/issue requests must reuse their original idempotency keys.
